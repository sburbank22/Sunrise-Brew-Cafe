Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Do you offer catering?

Yes, we provide catering for events of all sizes. Contact us at least 48 hours in advance for coffee service and pastry platters. We offer special rates for corporate and recurring orders.

Is there parking available?

Street parking is available on Morning Light Avenue and side streets. There's also a public parking garage one block east on Oak Street with two-hour free parking.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available for all customers. Ask any barista for the current password. We also have plenty of outlets for laptop users.

Can I book the space for events?

Our café is available for private events on Sunday evenings and Monday mornings. Contact us to discuss your needs and get a customized quote.

Do you sell whole bean coffee?

Absolutely. We sell our house blend and rotating single-origin coffees in 12oz bags. We can grind them to your specification or leave them whole for home grinding.

Are you hiring?

We're always looking for passionate people to join our team. Send your resume and a brief note about why you love coffee to jobs@sunrisebrew.cafe.
